# Live tuning overlay

The shell is a menu bar, a navigator stacked on an inspector in one side column, an optional help column, and a timeline strip. The menu bar is File, Edit, View, Project, Settings, and Help, and lists commands, checks, and the Settings and Project pages. The navigator holds only the visual composition, and the inspector holds only the settings rows of the selected page. Roots are Overlay, Post-FX, Pattern Mask, Timeline, and Layers. Settings and Project pages (`config_pages.py`) have no navigator row: their menu items open them in the inspector with a `Settings > Preview` style breadcrumb and leave no navigator row selected (`NavigatorControls.open_page`). Reconcile keeps an open config page; preset curation drops Project pages. Each layer has Milkdrop Preset, Preset Switching, Compositing, and Cleave Effects pages; Preset List sits one level under Preset Switching, not expandable, shown while the switching trigger is not Off. The user opens and closes navigator nodes with the twisty, Left/Right, and a click on the label. Selecting a node only opens its ancestors, and Layers starts open. Sub-rows draw tree lines in the indent gutter (`navigator_tree_rails`, `navigator_tree_lines`), and each stem drops from the parent's twisty. A click on an expandable label selects it; what else it does follows `editor.navigator_expand_click` (`navigator_label_click` in `shell_host.py`): `click-expand` (default) opens a closed row and a double-click closes an open one, `click-toggle` opens or closes it, and `button-only` leaves it to the twisty. The twisty always opens or closes with one click. Overlay, Post-FX, Pattern Mask, Timeline, and layer rows carry clickable on/off and lock icons (`NavNode.toggles`), plus solo on Overlay and layers, and each card row has its own on/off icon. Those pages list the same switches as value rows (`SECTION_SWITCH_KINDS`), and each card page opens with its own on/off row; other child pages have none. A click on an icon selects its row without opening or closing it. Preset curation has no icons and keeps the layer's text badge and lock mark. A child page's E, S, and L keys act on the parent that owns it (`node_control_descriptor`). Settings chooses a side for the navigator column and a side for help. The default puts the navigator column on the left and help on the right. When they share a side, help sits at the window edge and the navigator column stays against the visualiser. The navigator is only as tall as its rows, and the inspector fills the rest of the column and keeps at least half the band, until the user drags the navigator/inspector boundary (`editor.navigator_height`, 0 fits rows). A dragged height leaves each side at least one row. Help is an ImGui column. It does not cover the visualiser. The timeline band fits its rows until the user drags its top edge (`editor.timeline_height`, 0 fits rows); a taller band grows the rows, keeps the row header buttons and transport glyphs at the nominal row size, and leaves the columns at least two navigator rows. Column sides, stack width, help width, navigator height, timeline height, and UI scale are editor preferences. UI scale (`editor.ui_scale`, percent) drives every chrome font and spacing through `theme.ui_scale()`; read `tuning_ui_metrics()` / `timeline_ui_metrics()` / `ui_px()` at draw time, never at import. Layer number keys select the matching navigator row, and Ctrl+, opens Settings > Preview. Session state becomes view state; inspector pages come from section defs in `row_sections.py`. Warning and error toasts draw centered on the transport bar, only as wide as the message plus a space on each side. Enter, or a click on the toast, clears a timed toast. A pinned error has no click. When the selection changes, or a strip row selects a new clip (`timeline.selected_clip_serial`), and `editor.highlight_new_page` is on, the inspector title runs the same accent swipe (`AttentionClock`, `notification_attention`); the bar runs from the panel's left inset to a space before the title line's right-hand icons, and a title wider than its room scrolls to its end at reading pace, with the bar held long enough to pause before and after (`attention_scroll_sec`, `attention_scroll_hold_sec`, `attention_scroll_offset`). The title line ends in a flush-right cluster (`inspector_title_cluster`): the page's off and lock marks, then Back and Forward arrows. `PageHistory` (`page_history.py`, owned as `controls.page_history`) observes `selected_id` each tick and records a page after `PAGE_VISIT_DWELL_SEC`, up to `PAGE_HISTORY_LIMIT` entries. `NavigatorControls.go_back` / `go_forward` skip pages that can no longer show and keep the focus region, except that a non-strip page takes focus off the strip. Alt+Left / Alt+Right (`view.page_back` / `view.page_forward`) and mouse X1 / X2 step it too. Overlay Latin text uses bundled DejaVu Sans Mono; icons use Material Icons in `assets/fonts/`.

## Where new fields go

A new field is a page child or a command. Pages are `SectionDef`s in `row_sections.py`, mapped to navigator ids in `inspector.py` (`_PAGE_SECTIONS`, `_LAYER_PAGE_SECTIONS`):

- Settings: `SETTINGS_PREVIEW_SECTION`, `SETTINGS_UI_SECTION`
- Project: `PROJECT_MILKDROP_SECTION`, `PROJECT_COMPOSITOR_SECTION`, `PROJECT_RESOLUTION_SECTION` (render settings live in the Project > Render... dialog, `project_render_controls.py`)
- Overlay: `OVERLAY_PAGE_SECTION`, `OVERLAY_OPENING_CARD_SECTION`, `OVERLAY_CLOSING_CARD_SECTION`
- Post-FX: `POST_FX_PAGE_SECTION`, `POST_FX_FADE_SECTION`, `POST_FX_HIGHLIGHT_ROLLOFF_SECTION`, `POST_FX_CHROMA_BOOST_SECTION`, `POST_FX_LIMITER_SECTION`
- Pattern Mask: `PATTERN_MASK_PAGE_SECTION`, `PATTERN_MASK_PATTERN_SECTION`, `PATTERN_MASK_EDGES_SECTION`
- Timeline: `TIMELINE_PAGE_SECTION`, `BEAT_BAR_GRID_SECTION`, `TIMELINE_CUTS_SECTION`, `SONG_MARKERS_SECTION`
- Layers: `LAYERS_SECTION`, and per layer `TRACK_SECTION` (preset curation uses `CURATION_LAYER_SECTION`), `TRACK_MILKDROP_PRESET_SECTION`, `TRACK_PRESET_SWITCHING_SECTION`, `TRACK_PRESET_LIST_SECTION`, `TRACK_COMPOSITING_SECTION`, `TRACK_EFFECTS_SECTION`

A new page needs a navigator entry in `navigator.py` (or, for a Settings or Project page, an entry in `config_pages.CONFIG_PAGES`, which adds its menu item), a section mapping in `inspector.py`, and a header mapping in `row_hotkeys.py`.

The inspector has no open or closed state. A block nested in a page through `SectionNode.subsection` draws a static, non-navigable heading with its rows indented under it.

Conditional rows are siblings shown or hidden by a value predicate. The view-state structure includes or omits them at build time and rebuilds only when `view_state_structure_signature()` changes. A field that gates row presence belongs in that signature; leave value-only fields out. Add a `test_structure_signature_invalidates_on_*` test in `tests/cleave/viz/test_view_state_structure.py`.

New rows use `RowKind`, `RowSpec`, and `SectionNode`. Do not add a per-`RowKind` label or mutation branch in `controls.py`. Overlay chrome lives in `overlay_primitives`. `render_visibility_icon` stays in `row_present_renderers.py`.

The timeline strip stays its own host in `timeline_overlay.py`. Selecting a strip row (entering Timeline, or Up/Down there) sets `selected_id` to `timeline.row:<slot>` (`navigator.timeline_row_node_id`), an inspector page with no navigator row like a config page. It edits that row's selected clip (`TIMELINE_ROW_SECTION` holding `TIMELINE_CLIP_SECTION`, read from `TimelineBlock.selected_clips`). A clip is an on cue up to the next cue or the song end (`cleave.timeline.lane_clips`); lanes still store cues, clips are derived on demand, and the selection is the clip's start time (`timeline.selected_clip_t`). The heading shows the clip's span, the rows are Start, End, Opacity, Blend, Role, Cut in, and Cut out, and no selection shows no clip rows. Start and End trim by one snap step (one bar with the large step). Cut out is unavailable on a clip with no end cue of its own (it butts the next clip or runs to the song end). Which slots have a selected clip is in the structure signature. The strip's clip keys, these rows, and the clip context menu share `ClipEditor` (`timeline_clip_edits.py`, owned as `controls.clip_editor`); add a clip edit there, not in `TimelineControls`, as cue operations from `cleave/timeline.py` (`add_clip`, `remove_clip`, `move_clip`, `trim_clip`, `split_clip`, `update_clip`). Each edit records in `TimelineHistory` with a merge key, so a run of the same edit undoes as one step. A row with nothing to edit sets `RowSpec.available` and draws disabled. Closing the strip returns it to Timeline; navigator arrows start from Timeline. The focused strip row draws the shell's selected wash and focus outline.

## Parameterized actions

The inspector holds live properties. Commands are buttons. A command that needs parameters opens one dialog, and its label ends in "...". Nothing in the inspector waits for a later Apply.

A button is a command in `commands.py`, placed with `SectionNode(commands=...)` or `SectionDef.trailing_commands`. Commands that have no menu row go in `_INSPECTOR_COMMANDS`. `build_inspector_rows` moves every button row to the bottom of the page, in page order, under a `DIVIDER_ROW` (a thin, non-navigable rule shorter than a row; one also sits under the page title). `help_content._INSPECTOR_COMMAND_KINDS` maps a command to the `RowAffordance.COMMAND` row that holds its help text.

A property with a fixed list of options sets `RowSpec.choice` (`bool_choice` / `options_choice` in `row_specs/common.py`); a form field is a `FormField` with its options. The option count picks the control (`cleave/choice.py`): three or fewer draw as segments, more as a combo whose list opens on click or Enter (Space in a dialog). `RowChoice.combo` forces a combo for options too long to sit side by side. Lists order low to high, so Right means more, and every step clamps at the ends. On/off rows are false then true. Give a combo row a `jump` when one change must not repeat side effects. Numbers stay `< value >` steppers, padded to the widest value shown; in a dialog that is `FormField(stepper=True)` over the number's options, with `large_step` for PageUp/PageDown. The arrow glyphs come from `choice_layout` (`STEPPER_BACK`, `STEPPER_FORWARD`) and the hover from `choice_widgets.paint_stepper_hover`, shared by inspector rows and `draw_stepper`. Steps repeat while held, in both: keys through `KeyRepeatController`, arrows through `ValueNudgeHold` in the inspector and `ModalHost.press_form_step` / `release_form_step` in a dialog. `FormSpec.on_change` adjusts other draft values when one changes (keep a start before an end). Drawing is shared in `choice_widgets.py`; do not add a per-dialog control.

Dialogs with parameters use `ModalHost.prompt_form` with a `FormSpec`. Group related fields with `FormField.section`: the first field of each section draws a muted, non-focusable heading and sectioned rows indent under it. Fields edit draft values, so Cancel needs no restore. A dialog opens on the values last confirmed in it, kept for the session in its controller (or session runtime when other code reads them, like Generate's choices on `session.timeline`); only project settings, such as the render fps, persist. Use `info` to show what the draft would affect, from a dry run; it draws in yellow under the title, in brackets and lower case. Confirm-only dialogs use `prompt_confirm` with a verb label; `destructive=True` fills the button red and starts on Cancel. Any other dialog with fixed answers (Save, Don't save, Cancel, or a preset's roles) uses `prompt_buttons`, and a message with only OK uses `prompt_notice`. Every modal answer draws as a right-aligned button; there is no vertical option list.

Text and colour properties are `VALUE_TEXT` and `VALUE_COLOUR` rows. They edit in place through `controls.inline_edit`, which owns the keyboard while active. Multi-line text keeps the text modal. Every timeline edit records through `TimelineHistory`: `recording` for one that finishes at once, `begin` / `commit` / `cancel` for one that spans frames (a recorded take, a drag).

## Enabled gates the frame path

`enabled=False` on an Overlay, Post-FX, or Pattern Mask header must skip that block in the frame path, not only dim the inspector row. Overlay cards gate per card. Post-FX children must check `session.post_fx.enabled`. New post-FX subsections need an `enabled=False` test.

## Icon buttons

Every icon or arrow a press acts on goes through one button: geometry in `icon_button.py` (`icon_box`, `text_box`, `icon_columns`, `icon_row`, `glyph_x`, `icon_gap`), ImGui drawing and input in `imgui_icon_button.py` (`icon_button`, `box_button`, `paint_box`, `paint_wash`). A box is as tall as its row's highlight band (`imgui_theme.row_band` / `row_band_side`; a selectable's item rect is already that band), an icon's box is square, neighbours sit `icon_gap()` apart, and the glyph is centred. Callers pass the glyph, colours, fill, and enabled / inert state; never size, pad, or offset a box or glyph per site. The pygame strip builds its header surfaces from the same geometry.

## Typography

Colors live in `cleave/viz/theme.py`. Use existing roles (`LABEL`, `VALUE`, `DISABLED`, `MUTED`, `LOCKED`) plus the accent constants already there. Do not invent a new color without adding a theme constant.

Label+value rows: prefix in `LABEL`, value in the row value color. Exceptions (settings and project headers, transport, notifications, modals) are deliberate; match nearby rows.

## Mouse pointer

Cleave draws its own OS cursors (`pointer_cursor.py`): black with a white outline, sized by `POINTER_BASE_PX` times the UI scale. Draw code calls `request_pointer(PointerShape...)` inside the ImGui frame, from the same hover test that paints the control's highlight, so the pointer and the highlight agree and a modal blocks both. The request resets to the arrow every frame, the last call wins, and `ImGuiHost.end_frame` applies it. Never call `pygame.mouse.set_cursor` elsewhere.

- Arrow: the default, and anything that only selects (navigator and inspector rows, menus, list rows), plus disabled controls.
- Hand: a press acts now. Buttons, icon toggles, twisties, stepper arrows, segments and combos, colour swatches, the play/pause label, a clickable toast, file picker shortcuts, and the strip's play, REC, and row header buttons (arm, solo, eye, back to timeline). A strip row label only selects.
- Hand means the press would act right now, not only that the control is unlocked. A control that refuses for another reason keeps the arrow: `RowSpec.inert` for an inspector row, `NavToggle.inert` for a navigator icon, and `TimelineControls.strip_target_pressable` for the strip (the layer enabled switch while the timeline is on, REC with no armed row or a locked timeline, arm on a locked timeline, an eye on an unarmed row during a take, back to timeline during a take).
- Text: an editable text field.
- Resize EW / NS: column splitters, the navigator boundary, the timeline top edge, a value being dragged sideways, the transport time and bar, and the strip's time scale, a live scrub, its scrollbar while zoomed, or a clip edge (hovered or being trimmed).
- Move: a reorder handle, a row being dragged, a strip pan, and a strip clip body (hovered or being moved). Clip shapes come from `TimelinePointer.clip_gesture` and show only where clips can be edited. Not allowed: a blocked reorder handle.

Gestures that are not ImGui items (splitters, the strip, live drags) are requested in `ShellHost._request_gesture_pointer`, after the shell windows, so they win over widgets.

## Context menus

A right-click opens a context menu for what sits under the pointer; Shift+F10 or the Menu key opens one for the focused item. `ContextMenuState` (`context_menu.py`, no ImGui) holds the open menu, owned as `controls.context_menu`; `ContextMenuSurface` (`menu_rows.py`) draws it as a non-modal top-layer window, sharing `draw_menu_rows` with the menu-bar dropdown. A menu is a provider: a callable returning `ContextMenuItem`s, `ContextSubmenu`s (one level of child items, drawn beside the row by `ContextSubmenuSurface`), and `CONTEXT_SEPARATOR`, re-run every frame so labels, checks, and enabled state stay live. Items call public controller methods, the same paths as their keys, and name that key in `shortcut`. A disabled item stays listed and sets `hint` to say why. To give a region a menu, add a provider module (like `timeline_context_menu.py`), resolve it from the point in `ShellHost._context_menu_at`, and from the focus in `ShellHost._focus_context_menu`. Providers: `timeline_context_menu.py` (strip row; its selected clip's items come from `clip_context_menu.py`, which edits through `timeline_clip_edits.py`), `timeline_time_menu.py` (the clicked song time on the ruler or a gap on a row: seek, play, drop a song marker; on a marker, selected first, its items replace those: seek, play, a Type submenu, delete; the ruler adds Undo, Redo, and the timeline commands), `navigator_context_menu.py` (navigator row, hit from the row rects `ShellHost` records while drawing), `preview_context_menu.py` (the visualiser rect, a provider `ShellHost` takes from `app.py`: play/pause, a Solo layer submenu and Overlay/Post-FX/Pattern Mask on/off checks read from the navigator's icons, a Preview quality submenu, Grab still through `still_capture.py`, which saves the content framebuffer at its live size after the next composited frame, Play Full Screen, and Full Screen Editor; Shift+F10 opens it at the visualiser's top-left while Preview has focus), and `preset_context_menu.py` (a preset list entry, the current preset row, or the directory row's Jump to directory submenu in the inspector). `ShellHost` records inspector row rects by descriptor too; `_inspector_menu` maps a row kind to its provider, and a right-click focuses the row first. A right-click on a clip selects it first, and its items are Go to clip start, Split at playhead, Split here (at the right-clicked time; disabled when the menu opens from the keyboard), Cut in, Cut out, Role, and Delete clip, then Override visibility, Release all overrides, Arm for recording, and Solo layer (no Show / Hide layer, and Blend is left to the inspector); in a gap the menu leaves clip items out and leads with Add clip here (`clip_context_menu.add_clip_entry`), then the time items. Build an item from a menu-bar `Command` with `commands.command_menu_item`, so the label, shortcut, and enabled state match the menu bar. While a menu is open it takes every key, and a press outside it only closes it.

Each strip row's header is its label (number and stem), then `ROW_HEADER_BUTTONS` in `timeline_overlay.py`: arm, solo, eye, and back to timeline, each a square icon box one nominal row tall (`header_button_x`, `row_header_target_at`; draw with `render_header_toggle`). The clip bar runs to the right padding. Arm lights `ARMED_BG` (live-patched, flashing with REC during a take), solo lights `SOLO_BG` and calls the same `LayerMutations.enter_solo` / `exit_solo` as the navigator, and back to timeline shows only on an overridden row. Timeline visibility overrides start from the row's eye or number key (`TimelineControls.toggle_row_visibility`, which takes an override and flips it), the row menu, or Shift+Enter (`TimelineControls.toggle_override`), and end from back to timeline (`release_row_override`), the menu, or Shift+Enter; an overridden row's eye draws yellow (`OVERRIDE_BG`). An armed row has no override: arming releases one (`_toggle_arm`), and `toggle_override`, the eye, and the number key do not start one while it is armed, so its eye acts only during a take. There is no paused preview.

## Keyboard dispatch

Layered in `input_dispatch.py`. Modal keys first, then an open context menu and an active inline edit, each of which takes every key, then Shift+F10 / Menu. Then Tab / Shift+Tab cycles Menu, Navigator, Inspector, Preview, and Timeline. Timeline is skipped when the strip is closed. Preview (`ShellRegion.PREVIEW`) owns only Esc, back to the navigator: it draws the focus outline around the visualiser, Shift+F10 opens its menu, and a left or right click on the visualiser takes it. Then the menu bar, command shortcuts (Alt+Left / Alt+Right step page history), and Ctrl+Up / Ctrl+Down (previous / next top-level navigator node; no effect while the menu bar is focused). Navigator and inspector own their arrows, Enter, Esc, and PageUp/PageDown. An open strip does not take keys. Timeline takes arrows, Enter, Esc, and clip keys only while that region is active: Left/Right step between the row's clips, Enter seeks to the clip start, `,` and `.` nudge opacity, B and O cycle blend and role, C and Shift+C cycle cut in and cut out, Ctrl+K splits the clip under the playhead, and Delete turns the selected clip off. A left click on the open strip takes the Timeline region, then `TimelinePointer` (`timeline_pointer.py`) acts through `TimelineControls`, from `TimelineOverlay.hit_at`: a ruler press seeks, a row press focuses the row and selects the clip under the pointer without seeking, and a row double-click in a gap adds a clip (`TimelineControls.add_clip_at`, snapped, one bar long or up to the next clip); on a clip, or while clips cannot be edited, it seeks. `StripHit.clip_start` / `clip_edge` name the clip under the pointer and an edge within `CLIP_EDGE_HIT_PX` (an edge wins over the body; a boundary two clips share is the earlier clip's end). A held press on a clip that moves past `PAN_DRAG_PX` drags it through `ClipEditor.begin_drag` / `update_drag`: the body moves it between its neighbours, an edge trims it, and a shared edge rolls the boundary; release calls `end_drag` (one undo step) and Esc or a cancelled gesture calls `cancel_drag`, which restores the lane. Every update recomputes from the lane the drag began with. Mouse motion sets `timeline.strip_hover_clip`; the strip outlines the focused row's selected clip (the selection flash pulses the outline) and draws edge handles on the hovered or selected clip while clips can be edited. A press on a song marker (`StripHit.marker_index`) selects it through `TuningControls.select_song_marker`, which opens Song Markers with its row focused; on the ruler it also seeks to the marker, and on a row a clip under the pointer wins. Markers draw live through the ruler and rows, and the selected one flashes like a selected clip. A held ruler press or a held second click on a row's clip scrubs once per frame. The corner play/pause and record buttons call `toggle_transport` / `toggle_record` (Space / R). A press on a row label focuses the row. One press on a row header button acts: arm calls `toggle_arm_row` (A), solo `toggle_row_solo`, the eye `toggle_row_visibility` (the layer number key; during a take it acts only on an armed row), and back to timeline `release_row_override`. A right-click on a row or the ruler opens a context menu. Mouse motion sets `timeline.strip_hover`, which washes the hovered corner or row header button (`TimelineViewState.hover_row_target` for a row's; not the row label, which only selects, nor the empty back-to-timeline slot). The strip maps a visible time window onto its bar: `timeline.strip_view` (`StripView` in `timeline_view.py`, not persisted; the whole song until zoomed), copied to `TimelineViewState.view_start_sec` / `view_end_sec`. Draw and hit code map time through `time_to_x` / `x_to_time` with that window and skip anything outside it; never map against the song duration. The wheel over the strip goes to `TimelinePointer.wheel` instead of ImGui: it zooms about the time under the pointer, and Shift or a sideways wheel pans. While zoomed, a left drag on a gap in a row past `PAN_DRAG_PX` or a middle drag on the ruler or a row pans, and the scrollbar under the rows (`scroll_thumb` / `scroll_track` hits) drags the window. `StripView.follow_playhead` runs once per frame: playback pages the window forward, a paused seek recentres it, and a held gesture (`StripView.held`) suspends it. Play Full Screen (`WindowControls.play_fullscreen`) runs after modals and an open context menu, before every other layer: Esc and Ctrl+F leave, Space and the seek keys act, Ctrl+Q quits, and every other key is swallowed; the shell draws only an open context menu (`ShellHost.draw_play_frame`), a right-click opens `play_fullscreen_menu` (Play/Pause, Exit Full Screen) through `ShellHost.process_play_event`, the present rect is the whole window, and an open modal or picker leaves it. `h` toggles the help column and stays global. Opening or closing help does not move focus. The help column is not a Tab stop, and a click in it does not move focus. There is no panel region and no main-list focus ring.

`row_hotkeys.handle_row_hotkey` is the per-descriptor path for letter, Delete, and Backspace keys, used by the navigator and the inspector.
